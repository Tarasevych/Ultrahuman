Hello Ultrahuman team,

I am a Ukrainian-speaking Ultrahuman user and would like to contribute a volunteer Ukrainian localization package for the Ultrahuman Android app.

I created a public, transparent repository with target Ukrainian resources, integration notes, and import-friendly target-only formats:

https://github.com/Tarasevych/Ultrahuman

The repository does not contain APK files, extracted proprietary binaries, work logs, or a public full English source-string workbook. It contains Ukrainian resources and resource-key-based formats intended for official review and integration by your team.

My goal is to support a product I personally use and help make Ultrahuman more accessible to Ukrainian users. If Ukrainian can be enabled in a test build or screenshots, I can continue volunteer QA and submit follow-up wording fixes based on the real app UI.

I would also be glad to help with Ukrainian localization of articles, recommendations, and other server-driven content if your team can provide the preferred source format or localization platform workflow.
